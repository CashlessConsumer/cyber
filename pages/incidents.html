How this ledger works

Rows land when a claim becomes public or when we can independently verify one. Status chips follow our claim rubric (L0–L5): a leak-site listing is a claim until the entity confirms, the corpus is verified, or both. We do not republish leaked data; verification is at metadata level. Gov-adjacent rows (police, government mail) are tracked when the actor overlaps with banking-sector claims.
