Agentic work Human-supervised AI on Zo Computer ▸ what this means

Everything on this site is agentic cyber work: AI agents running on Zo Computer plan the probes, execute read-only scans, analyse the results and draft the findings — predominantly on GLM 5.3 Flash — the cheapest flash-tier model available, run with Zero Data Retention. A human steers and monitors every stage. No scan runs, no finding is disclosed and no text is published without human review and sign-off.

The machine does the legwork at speed and scale; the intent, the ethics and the accountability are human.
